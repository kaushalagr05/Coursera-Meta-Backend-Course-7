Little Lemon Story

Chicago, Illinois

Based in Chicago, Illinois, Little Lemon is a family-owned Mediterranean restaurant, focused on traditional recipes served with a modern twist. The chefs Adrian and Mario draw inspiration from Italian, Greek, and Turkish culture and have developed a menu of delicious rustic dishes and specialty drinks.

Our mission is to bring you warmth, hospitality, and unforgettable meals prepared with genuine passion and the highest quality ingredients.
